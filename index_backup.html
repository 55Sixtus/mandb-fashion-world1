About Us

Where Elegance Meets Excellence

At M&B Fashion World, we believe fashion is more than clothing—it is confidence, culture, and timeless elegance. For over 8 years, we have specialized in creating luxurious custom African fashion for clients across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Germany, Ireland, and Europe.

Every garment is handcrafted in Benin City, Nigeria, by skilled artisans using premium fabrics, expert tailoring, and exceptional attention to detail. Whether you're dressing for a wedding, church event, birthday celebration, traditional ceremony, or corporate occasion, every piece is made exclusively to your measurements and personal style.

From your first consultation to worldwide delivery, our commitment is simple: create beautifully tailored outfits that fit perfectly, celebrate African heritage, and leave a lasting impression wherever you go.

Privacy Policy

M&B Fashion World respects your privacy. We collect name, email, phone, measurements, and photos only to create your custom garment. We do not share your data with third parties. Payment info is processed by PayPal/Bank and not stored by us. Contact: beckydominic133@gmail.com to request data deletion.

Refund & Returns Policy

All garments are custom made to your measurements. Due to this, we do not offer refunds. We offer 1 free remake if the fit is incorrect due to our error within 7 days of receiving the item. Please contact us immediately if you experience any issues.

Terms of Service

By placing an order with M&B Fashion World, you agree to our processing and shipping timelines. Custom orders require a 50% deposit upfront, with the balance due before shipping. We are not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance in your country.
